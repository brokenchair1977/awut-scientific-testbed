A primer for understanding AWUT (A Working Unified Theory) without requiring a PhD in physics.

## **What Problem Does AWUT Solve?**

The Standard Model of particle physics is incredibly successful, but it has **19 free parameters** for particle masses that must be measured experimentally - they cannot be predicted from theory.

Additionally, astrophysical observations show that:
- Galaxies rotate faster than visible matter alone predicts
- Gravitational lensing is stronger than visible matter suggests

This led to the "dark matter" hypothesis - invisible matter that makes up 85% of the universe.

**AWUT's claim:** We can derive particle masses AND explain "dark matter" from just **4 primitives**.

## **How Does This Explain Particle Masses?**

Particles are stable **resonances** in the bubble structure.

**Analogy:** Musical instruments
- Guitar string: Different frequencies (notes) come from the same string
- Particles: Different masses come from different resonance patterns in the same bubble structure

**Example: Leptons (Electron, Muon, Tau)**

All three are the same fundamental object, just in different "harmonic modes":

```
Electron: Ground state (n = 1)          → mass ≈ 0.511 MeV
Muon:     First excitation (n ≈ 5)     → mass ≈ 105.7 MeV
Tau:      Second excitation (n ≈ 8)    → mass ≈ 1777 MeV
```

Mass ratio:
```
m_muon / m_electron = (f_Blue / f_Red)^5 ≈ 207
```

This is **derived**, not fitted!

## **Nuclear Binding Energies**

Atomic nuclei are bound by the strong nuclear force. The binding energy determines:
- Nuclear stability
- Energy released in fission/fusion
- Element abundances in stars

Standard approach: Semi-Empirical Mass Formula (5 fitted parameters)

**AWUT approach:**
