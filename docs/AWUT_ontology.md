### **The 3 Bubble Types**

AWUT posits that spacetime is composed of three types of topological "bubbles":

#### **1. Blue Bubbles**
- **Frequency:** f_Blue = 12.37 GHz
- **Wavelength:** λ_Blue = c/f_Blue ≈ 2.42 cm
- **Role:** High-energy fermions (protons, heavy particles)
- **Charge:** Positive in electromagnetic interactions
- **Stability:** Stable in isolation

#### **2. Red Bubbles**
- **Frequency:** f_Red = 9.22 GHz
- **Wavelength:** λ_Red = c/f_Red ≈ 3.25 cm
- **Role:** Low-energy fermions (electrons, light particles)
- **Charge:** Negative in electromagnetic interactions
- **Stability:** Stable in isolation

#### **3. Purple Wells**
- **Formation:** Created at the intersection of Blue and Red bubbles
- **Density:** ρ_Purple ∝ 1/ℓ_PS³
- **Role:**
  - Local vacuum structure
  - "Dark matter" replacement
  - Gravitational lensing medium
  - Muon g-2 modulation
- **Stability:** Metastable, depends on local Blue/Red configuration
